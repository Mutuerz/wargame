## Premise

A long river border splits two powers. Every crossing has been blown except two bridges, one held by each side.
Whoever controls the ground between them, the strip both armies call **the Bridgehead**, controls the river, and
with it the war. Neither side can afford to bring in heavy armies, so each sends small elite companies of ten,
resupplied by truck across their own bridge and reinforced by parachute. _(in game: two bases, two bridges, supply
trucks, parachute respawns, five sectors in the neutral zone)_

The fighting rolls across five theatres along the river: woods, desert, a ruined city, frozen pine forest and swamp.
_(in game: the five theatres)_

## Tone

Grounded and gritty, close to a modern military shooter, but the story stays in the background: it explains why the
match exists, it does not interrupt it. Short radio-style lines are enough.
